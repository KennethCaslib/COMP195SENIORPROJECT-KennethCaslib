Brand Photography

At the end of 2021, a fellow colleague and I had an idea of starting a clothing brand. This consisted of him designing all of the clothes, while I would handle all of the photos and videos in regards to advertising the merchandise. This brand is known as ‘Bystndrs’ and was started here in Stockton, CA. We are currently over a year strong, selling products all around the United States and Internationally. We have formed business relationships with large companies and celebrities from all around the world, as well as forming a team of ambassadors from California.
