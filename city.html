City Photography

As a photographer, there may be moments where the camera will barely be picked up and will catch dust. I try my best to avoid these times by constantly bringing my camera with me wherever I go. If I am going out with friends or family, or even doing simple errand run, I try my best to capture every moment. I am a person that enjoys the outdoors tremendously, especially if it is day trips in another city or even walking around the city of Stockton. I try to utilize these moments and capture anything and everything that catches my eye. My most favorite will be capturing night lights within a large city, such as San Francisco.
