Get to know me!

My name is Kenneth Caslib and I am a freelance photographer and videographer located in Stockton, California. I have been pursuing photography for nearly 7 years now and videography for 3 years. I am currently studying Computer Science at University of the Pacific.

Over the course of the last few years, I have been able to learn about numerous techniques when it comes to this specific work of art. I have surrounded myself with various photographers and videographers all around Stockton and California, spending countless amount of hours studying their work and learning different techniques from them. This all lead to my large clientele that slowly started to rise over the course of the years. From my fellow peers spreading my work, allowing for their friends and family to reach out. This eventually led to small businesses all around Stockton and California reaching out to me in regards to creating content.

I am very open to working with my clientele and new clients, and always ensuring that I perfect their needs when it comes to this art. Additionally, I always ensure that they are extremely satisfied with the work I provide them. Constantly always asking questions allows me to further gain knowledge of what their eye seeks.

Please do not hesitate to reach out for any questions! I am always open, whether it is photography or videography or even computer science!
