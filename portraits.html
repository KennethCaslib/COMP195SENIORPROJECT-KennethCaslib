Portrait Photography

Throughout my years of photography, I have been able to strengthen my portrait skills because of many beautiful individuals that have allowed me to capture special moments. Many of these portraits consist of graduation photos, prom, birthday, or business modeling. After every photoshoot, I have allowed myself to learn new skills each time and implement them into the next. This also allows me to learn any mistakes that I may have which will allow me to grow from them.
